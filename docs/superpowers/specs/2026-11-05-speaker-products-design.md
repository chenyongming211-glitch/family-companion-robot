User requests loading commercial or open-source products, comparing recognition with our baseline, and deriving product optimization. Latest direction: if an open-source module performs better and its license fits, integrate that module instead of developing a new speaker algorithm. This authorizes the bounded loading/evaluation below; production adoption remains contingent on evidence. Do not reopen Eagle registration or vendor outreach.
